# Organization Settings

Access organization settings at **Settings** in the sidebar. The Settings page has several tabs:

---

## Organization Tab

Edit your organization's core business details:

- **Business Name**: the name displayed across the app and on postcards
- **Registration Number**: optional legal/tax registration number
- **Industry**: your business category
- **Business Address**: your official address (used as the return address on postcards)
- **Email**: primary contact email
- **Phone**: business phone number
- **Website**: your website URL

Click **Save** after making changes.

---

## Branding Tab

Set your organization's visual identity — this affects postcard templates that use brand variables:

- **Logo**: upload your company logo (PNG or SVG recommended)
- **Primary Color**: main brand color (used for headers and accents in templates)
- **Secondary Color**: supporting brand color
- **Accent Color**: call-to-action and highlight color
- **Heading Font**: the primary font used for headlines in templates

Changes to branding here automatically update any template elements using brand variable references.

---

## Subscription/Payments Tab

Manage payment methods and view billing history. See the Billing and Payments guide for full details.

---

## Preferences Tab

- **Timezone**: sets how dates and times are displayed throughout the app
- **Email preferences**: control which notification emails you receive
- **Notification settings**: in-app notification preferences

---

## Team / User Management Tab

View and manage team members. See the Team Management guide for full details.

---

## Deleting Your Organization

The Danger Zone section at the bottom of Settings contains the organization deletion option.

**Only the Owner can delete the organization.**

To delete:
1. Click **Delete Organization** in the Danger Zone
2. Type your exact organization name to confirm (case-sensitive)
3. Click Confirm Delete

**What happens after deletion is initiated:**
- Deletion is **scheduled 30 days from now** — it does not happen immediately
- You will receive a recovery email with instructions to cancel if it was a mistake
- During the 30-day window, a recovery banner appears in the app — click it to cancel the deletion
- All team members retain access during the 30-day window
- After 30 days, deletion is **permanent and irreversible**: all campaigns, zones, templates, addresses, referrals, and analytics data are deleted

**To cancel a scheduled deletion:**
- Click the recovery banner that appears at the top of the app, OR
- Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Cancel Scheduled Deletion

If the 30-day window has passed, the data cannot be recovered. Contact support immediately if you believe a deletion was initiated in error.
