# Campaign Launch and Payment

## Before You Can Launch

Three things must be in place before DoorKnocker will let you launch:

1. **Verified addresses**: the zone's addresses must have been validated for deliverability, or (for address list campaigns) verification must be completed or explicitly skipped
2. **A payment method on file**: Settings → Subscription/Payments → add a card
3. **Admin role or higher**: Marketers cannot process payments and cannot launch campaigns — only Admins and Owners can

---

## Paper Quality

At the launch step, you choose between two paper options:

| Option | Price Per Postcard |
|---|---|
| Standard | $3.00 |
| Premium | $3.50 |

Premium paper is heavier stock with a higher-quality finish. The choice is made per campaign at launch — you can't change it afterward.

---

## How the Cost Is Calculated

**Total cost = verified address count × price per postcard**

Only addresses that passed verification are included. Addresses that failed verification or were flagged as undeliverable are excluded from the count and are not charged.

Example: If your zone has 200 addresses, 185 verified and 15 undeliverable, and you choose Standard paper:
- You're charged for 185 postcards × $3.00 = **$555.00**
- The 15 undeliverable addresses are excluded at no cost

---

## Coupon Codes

If you have a coupon code, enter it in the coupon code field on the review step before clicking Launch. The discount will be shown as a line item on the final cost summary and on the invoice.

---

## Payment Methods

You must have at least one credit or debit card on file to launch. Add cards at **Settings → Subscription/Payments**. Only Admins and Owners can add or remove payment methods.

The default card on file is used at checkout. If you have multiple cards, the default is charged unless you change your default card in Settings first.

---

## After You Click Launch

- You are charged immediately for the verified address count
- The postcards enter the print queue — no cancellation or refund is available at this point
- The campaign status changes to **Active**
- Delivery tracking begins appearing on the campaign detail page within 1–2 business days
- Print and delivery typically takes **3–7 business days** end-to-end

---

## Who Can Launch

| Role | Can Launch? |
|---|---|
| Owner | Yes |
| Admin | Yes |
| Marketer | No — Marketers cannot process payments |
| Technician | No |

If you're getting an error when trying to launch, contact your organization's Admin to either launch for you or upgrade your role.
