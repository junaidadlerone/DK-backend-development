## Notes

## Agency Overview
Available only to Agency-type accounts (selected during onboarding).
Manage multiple client organizations from a single dashboard.

## Features
- Multi-client campaign management across all client organizations
- Client-level analytics and tracking
- Agency team management (/agency/team)
- Shared template library (/agency/templates)

## Business vs Agency
- **Business**: Single organization, direct campaign management
- **Agency**: Parent org managing multiple client sub-organizations
- Type is selected at onboarding and cannot be changed

## Getting Started as an Agency
1. Complete agency onboarding (workspace type → AgencyBasics → invite team)
2. Create client organizations from the Agency Overview
3. Switch to a client org to manage their campaigns
4. Use /agency/templates for shared postcard templates
