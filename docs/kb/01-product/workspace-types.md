# Workspace Types: Business vs Agency

When you first sign up for DoorKnocker, you choose a workspace type during onboarding. This choice determines how your account is structured and what features are available to you.

## The Two Types

### Business Workspace
Best for a single company running its own campaigns.

- One organization with one set of campaigns, zones, templates, and team members
- No multi-client management — everything is scoped to your one business
- Simpler sidebar navigation (no agency-specific pages)

### Agency Workspace
Best for marketing agencies managing campaigns on behalf of multiple clients.

- A parent "agency" organization that can contain multiple client sub-organizations
- Each client organization is fully isolated — its own campaigns, zones, templates, team members, and billing
- Agency-level pages: Agency Overview, Agency Team, Agency Templates
- Shared templates: create a postcard design at the agency level and share it with specific client orgs
- An organization switcher in the top navigation bar lets you jump between your agency and any client

## Choosing Between Them

Choose **Business** if you are running campaigns for your own company only.

Choose **Agency** if you need to manage campaigns for two or more separate businesses from a single login.

## Important: Workspace Type Cannot Be Changed

Your workspace type is locked in after you complete onboarding. There is no self-serve way to switch from Business to Agency or vice versa after the fact. If you chose the wrong type, contact support.

## How Organization Switching Works (Agency)

Once you are in an agency workspace:
1. Click the organization name in the top navigation bar
2. A dropdown shows your agency organization and all client organizations you manage
3. Click any organization to switch to it — the sidebar, campaigns, and all data update to show that org's context
4. Your user account and login session remain the same; only the active organization context changes

All data — campaigns, zones, templates, analytics — is fully isolated per organization. Switching orgs never mixes data between clients.
