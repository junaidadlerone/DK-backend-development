# Analytics Dashboard

**URL**: /analytics

## Sub-sections
- **Overview**: High-level KPIs — total sent, total spend, delivery rate
- **Campaign Performance**: Per-campaign send counts and delivery data
- **ROI & Cost Analytics**: Cost per postcard, cost per lead, estimated ROI
- **Engagement Deep Dive**: Detailed engagement and geographic breakdowns

## Date Filtering
All views support date range filtering. Default: last 30 days.
Navigate via the Analytics item in the left sidebar.
