# Postcard Sizes and Pricing

## Available Sizes

DoorKnocker supports three standard postcard sizes:

| Size | Dimensions | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 4 × 6 inches | Simple designs, cost-sensitive campaigns |
| Medium | 6 × 9 inches | Most popular; good balance of space and impact |
| Large | 6 × 11 inches | Maximum design space; high-impact campaigns |

The size you choose affects the physical size of the printed postcard. It does not affect the per-postcard price — pricing is the same regardless of size.

**You cannot change the size after a template is created.** If you need a different size, duplicate the template and set the new size when creating the duplicate.

---

## Per-Postcard Pricing

At campaign launch, you choose one of two paper quality options:

| Paper Quality | Price Per Postcard |
|---|---|
| Standard | $3.00 |
| Premium | $3.50 |

**Standard**: Regular postcard stock, suitable for most campaigns.

**Premium**: Heavier, higher-quality paper with a more refined finish — best for premium services or high-value client impressions.

---

## How Total Cost Is Calculated

**Total cost = number of verified, deliverable addresses × price per postcard**

You are charged **only for addresses that pass verification**. Unverified, invalid, and opt-out addresses are excluded and not charged.

Example:
- Zone with 300 addresses
- 270 verified, 30 unverifiable
- Premium paper ($3.50)
- Total: 270 × $3.50 = **$945.00**

There is **no minimum order quantity**. You can launch a campaign targeting as few as 1 verified address.

---

## Coupon Codes

If you have a promotional code, enter it at the review step during campaign launch. The discount is applied as a line item and reduces the total before charging.

---

## Sandbox Mode Pricing

In Sandbox mode, all campaigns cost a flat **$3.00** regardless of address count, paper quality, or postcard size. No real postcards are printed in sandbox — this is purely a testing environment. See the Sandbox Mode guide for details.

---

## Invoices and Billing History

Every campaign launch generates a transaction record in your billing history (Settings → Subscription/Payments). You can download a PDF invoice for any transaction from that page.
