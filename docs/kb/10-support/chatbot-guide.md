# About This Assistant

The chat assistant you're using right now is built into DoorKnocker to help you get answers without leaving the app.

## What the Assistant Can Help With

- **Feature questions**: "How do I create a targeting zone?" / "What does Premium paper mean?"
- **Step-by-step guidance**: walking you through any workflow in the app
- **Explaining concepts**: "What's the difference between radius and count mode?"
- **Troubleshooting**: "My campaign won't launch — what should I check?"
- **Understanding errors**: interpreting error messages you see in the app
- **Role and permissions questions**: "Can a Marketer launch campaigns?"
- **Billing and pricing questions**: "How much does it cost per postcard?"

## What the Assistant Cannot Do

- **It cannot take actions in your account.** It can't create campaigns, zones, or templates for you — it can only tell you how.
- **It cannot see your data.** The assistant has no visibility into your campaigns, addresses, or billing history.
- **It cannot process refunds or resolve billing disputes.** Those require the support team.
- **It cannot contact the support team on your behalf.** It can direct you to support, but it cannot submit a ticket for you.

## How to Get the Best Answers

**Be specific.** The more detail you provide, the better the answer:

- Less useful: "It's not working"
- More useful: "I'm trying to launch a campaign and I'm getting an error that says 'All consents must be provided' but I can't find what I'm missing"

**Include context**: mention what page you're on, what you're trying to do, and what you see happening instead.

## If the Assistant Can't Answer

The assistant will tell you honestly when it doesn't know something. If it can't answer your question, it will suggest contacting the DoorKnocker support team directly. The support team can handle account-specific issues, billing disputes, and anything that requires looking at your actual account data.

## Feedback

After a few messages, you'll see a rating prompt. Please leave a rating — it helps improve the assistant over time. You can also leave a short note about what was helpful or what could be better.

## Session Memory

The assistant does not remember previous chat sessions. Each time you open the chat widget, it starts fresh. If you're continuing a conversation from earlier, you may need to briefly re-explain the context.

Within a single session (as long as you keep the chat widget open), the assistant remembers everything said in that session.
