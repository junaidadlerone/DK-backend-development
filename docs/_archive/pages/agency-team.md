## Notes

## Agency Team Management
Manage team members at the agency level. These members have access across all client organizations (based on role).

## Roles at Agency Level
- **Admin**: Full access to all agency clients and settings
- **Marketer**: Can manage campaigns for assigned clients
- **Technician**: Operational access

## Inviting Agency Team Members
1. Click "Invite Member"
2. Enter email and select role
3. They get access to the agency and any clients they're assigned to
