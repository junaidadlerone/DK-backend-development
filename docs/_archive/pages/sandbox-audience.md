## Notes

## Sandbox Mode
A testing environment for campaign prototyping. Flat $3 fee regardless of address count.
No real postcards are sent. Use it to test targeting, maps, and templates.

## How to Use
1. Navigate to /sandbox/campaign-audience
2. Define your audience (address, radius, filters)
3. Preview estimated reach on the map
4. No billing per postcard — just the $3 flat fee

## Sandbox vs Live Campaign
| | Sandbox | Live |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | $3 flat | Per-postcard rate |
| Postcards sent | No | Yes |
| Address validation | Optional | Required |
| CSV export | No | Yes |
