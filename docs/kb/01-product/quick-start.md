# Quick Start Guide

## Step 1: Create an Account

Go to the DoorKnocker sign-up page and create an account with your email and a password, or sign in with Google. After signing up, check your email for a verification link — you must verify your email before you can access the app.

## Step 2: Complete Onboarding

After email verification, you'll walk through a short onboarding flow. The steps differ depending on your workspace type:

**Business workspace:**
1. Choose "Business" as your workspace type
2. Enter your business details (name, industry, phone, website)
3. Enter your business address
4. Upload your logo and set your brand colors
5. (Optional) Invite team members

**Agency workspace:**
1. Choose "Agency" as your workspace type
2. Enter your agency name, agency type, and website
3. (Optional) Invite team members

Onboarding is required before you can access the main app. Each step must be completed in order.

## Step 3: Explore the Dashboard

After onboarding, you'll land on the Dashboard, which shows a summary of recent campaign activity, stats, and quick-action buttons. The sidebar on the left is your main navigation.

## Your First Campaign in 5 Steps

1. Go to **Targeting → Zones** and click "+ Create Zone" to define a geographic area
2. Enter an address or coordinates as your center point, choose radius or count mode, and click "Find Addresses"
3. Click "Create Zone" to save it
4. Go to **Campaigns** and click "+ New Campaign"
5. Name your campaign, select your zone, choose a template, review the cost, and click Launch

## Key Concepts Glossary

| Term | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Zone** | A saved geographic area with a list of target addresses |
| **Verification** | The process of checking that each address can receive mail |
| **Template** | A saved postcard design (front + back) |
| **Campaign** | A zone + template combination that gets launched and mailed |
| **Referral** | A homeowner job record used to create a campaign targeting that job's neighborhood |
| **Analytics** | Delivery tracking, QR scan rates, cost per lead, ROI estimates |
| **Sandbox** | A test mode for experimenting with targeting and pricing without real sends |

## Where to Go After Signing Up

- **No campaigns yet?** → Start at Targeting → Zones to define your first target area
- **Have a design in mind?** → Go to Templates to build your postcard first
- **Want to estimate costs first?** → Try the Sandbox from the top navigation
- **Managing multiple clients?** → Go to Agency Overview to add your first client organization
