# User Profile and Account Settings

## Accessing Your Profile

Click your name or avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to open your profile settings.

---

## What You Can Edit

- **Full name**: the name shown to your teammates in the Team list
- **Profile photo**: upload a photo to personalize your account

---

## Changing Your Password

1. Go to your Profile
2. Scroll to the Password section
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter your new password
5. Confirm the new password
6. Click **Update Password**

**Password requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter
- Must include at least one lowercase letter
- Must include at least one number
- Must include at least one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $)

---

## Email Address

Your email address **cannot be changed from the profile page**. It is tied to your account identity. If you need to change your login email, contact DoorKnocker support.

---

## Organization Switching

If you belong to multiple organizations (e.g., you were invited to a client org and an agency org), you can switch between them:

- Click the organization name in the top navigation bar (if you're in an agency workspace)
- Or use the sidebar dropdown (in some views)

Switching organizations changes the active context — campaigns, zones, and data shown will be from the newly selected organization.

---

## Logging Out

To log out, click **Log Out** at the bottom of the sidebar (usually near your avatar/name). You'll be taken to the login screen.

Your active session data (chat history, draft campaigns) is preserved — drafts remain as Drafts and can be continued after you log back in.

---

## Account Security

- Enable a strong unique password and do not share login credentials with teammates — invite them separately via Team Management instead
- If you believe your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact support
- Sessions expire after a period of inactivity — you may be prompted to log in again
