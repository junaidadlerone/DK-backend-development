# Creating a Campaign: Step by Step

## Starting a New Campaign

Go to **Campaigns** in the sidebar and click **+ New Campaign** (or the equivalent button). You'll enter a multi-step creation flow.

---

## Step 1: Name Your Campaign

Enter a name for this campaign. This is an internal label — it is not printed on the postcard. Choose a name that helps you identify this campaign later (e.g., "East Austin Roofing June 2026" or "Smith Job Neighborhood Blitz").

Campaign names must be unique within your organization.

---

## Step 2: Choose Targeting

This step differs by campaign type:

**Location Zone**: Select an existing zone from the list, or click "Create New Zone" to define one on the map. The zone determines which addresses will receive postcards.

**Address List**: Upload a CSV file. The system will process it, let you map column names if needed, and validate the addresses.

**Referral**: Select from your existing referral records. The referral's address becomes the center of the campaign's targeting zone.

---

## Step 3: Choose a Template

Browse your organization's template library and select the postcard design you want to use. If you don't have a template yet, click "Create New Template" to open the template editor.

Templates come in three sizes: 4×6, 6×9, and 6×11 inches. The size affects the visual layout but not the per-postcard price.

---

## Step 4: Review and Launch

The final step shows you:
- **Address count**: how many verified, deliverable addresses will receive a postcard
- **Paper quality selection**: Standard ($3.00/postcard) or Premium ($3.50/postcard)
- **Total cost**: address count × price per postcard
- **Coupon code field**: enter a code to apply a discount
- **Payment method**: the card on file that will be charged
- **Consents**: checkboxes confirming you agree to the mailing terms — all must be checked to launch

Click **Launch Campaign** to confirm. You will be charged immediately and the postcards enter the print queue.

---

## Draft Auto-Save

Your campaign is automatically saved as a Draft after completing Step 1. If you navigate away, you can find it in the Campaigns list under status "Draft" and continue from where you left off.

---

## Validation Rules

The system will not allow you to launch if:
- The campaign has no name
- No zone or address list is attached
- No template is selected
- Not all consent checkboxes are checked
- No payment method is on file

---

## After Launch

- The campaign status changes from Draft to **Active**
- Postcards enter the print queue and cannot be canceled or refunded
- If the campaign is a Referral type, the linked referral status changes to **In Use**
- You can track delivery progress from the campaign detail page
