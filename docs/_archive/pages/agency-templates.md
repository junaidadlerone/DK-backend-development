## Notes

## Agency Templates
Shared postcard templates that can be used across all client organizations.
Create a template here once and reuse it for any client campaign.

## How to Use
1. Create or upload a template here
2. When creating a campaign for a client org, select from agency templates
3. Templates created at client level are private to that client

## Benefits
- Design consistency across clients
- Save time — build once, use everywhere
- Brand-locked templates prevent client-level modifications
