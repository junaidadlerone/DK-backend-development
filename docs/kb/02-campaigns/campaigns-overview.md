# Campaigns Overview

A campaign is the core unit of work in DoorKnocker. It pairs a targeting zone (or address list) with a postcard template, launches the mailing, and tracks results.

## The Campaigns List Page

Navigate to **Campaigns** in the sidebar to see all your campaigns. At the top of the page, four KPI cards give you an at-a-glance summary:

- **Active Campaigns**: how many campaigns are currently in Active status
- **Total Postcards Sent**: cumulative count across all campaigns
- **Avg Scan Rate**: average QR code scan rate across all campaigns
- **Total Spent**: total amount charged across all launched campaigns

Below the KPI cards is a table showing all campaigns with columns for name, status, address count, sent date, and spend.

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar to find campaigns by name. Use the status filter to show only Draft, Active, Inactive, or Archived campaigns.

## Campaign Detail Page

Click any campaign in the list to open the detail page. You'll see:

- **Stat cards**: total sent, delivery rate, QR scans, total cost
- **Map**: shows the targeting zone with address markers color-coded by delivery status
- **Address table**: every address in the campaign with its individual delivery status
- **Postcard preview**: front and back of the postcard design used
- **Send history**: log of all sends associated with this campaign

## Campaign Actions

| Action | When Available |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Draft status only — once launched, a campaign cannot be edited |
| **Duplicate** | Any status — creates a new Draft with the same zone and template |
| **Archive** | Any status — hides the campaign from the default list view |

## Can I Edit a Launched Campaign?

No. Once a campaign is launched, the postcards enter the print and mail queue immediately. Editing is disabled to prevent inconsistencies between what was ordered and what is shown. If you need to make changes, duplicate the campaign to create a new editable draft.
