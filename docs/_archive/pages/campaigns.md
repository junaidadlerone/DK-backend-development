## Notes

## Campaigns List
Lists all campaigns. Columns: Name, Status, Postcard count, Send date, Created.
Click a row for campaign detail. Click "Create Campaign" to start a new one.

## Campaign Statuses
- Draft: created, not sent
- Scheduled: queued for future send
- Sending: in progress
- Sent: dispatched
- Archived: removed from active list
