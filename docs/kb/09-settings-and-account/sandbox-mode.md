# Sandbox Mode

Sandbox is a safe testing environment built into DoorKnocker. It lets you explore campaign targeting, estimate audience sizes, and preview costs — without spending real money or sending actual postcards.

## Accessing Sandbox

Click **Sandbox** in the top navigation bar, or go directly to the sandbox URL. You'll see the Sandbox Campaign Audience Builder.

---

## What Sandbox Does

The sandbox gives you an interactive map-based audience builder with advanced features:

- **Map exploration**: pan and zoom to any area in the US
- **Address point selection**: see individual address points on the map
- **Draw-on-map targeting**: draw a polygon on the map to select addresses in any custom shape — not just circles
- **Advanced filters**:
  - Homeowner vs renter status
  - Property type (single family, multi-family, condo)
  - Business categories
  - Income range
  - Years in residence
- **Budget targeting**: set a target spend and the system selects the optimal number of addresses
- **Mailing list review**: preview the full address list before deciding to launch

---

## Sandbox Pricing

All sandbox campaigns cost a **flat $3.00** — regardless of how many addresses you select, what paper quality you choose, or what postcard size you use. This is a test fee, not a per-postcard charge.

**No real postcards are printed or mailed in sandbox mode.** The $3.00 charge is the only cost.

---

## Differences Between Sandbox and Live Campaigns

| Feature | Sandbox | Live Campaign |
|---|---|---|
| Postcards printed and mailed | No | Yes |
| Cost | Flat $3.00 | Per verified address ($3.00 or $3.50) |
| Address verification | Not run (mock data) | Full verification |
| Analytics data | Mock/test data | Real delivery and scan data |
| CSV export | Not available | N/A |
| Draw-on-map targeting | Available | Not available (radius/count only) |
| Advanced demographic filters | Available | Not available |

---

## When to Use Sandbox

- **Before your first campaign**: understand how addressing works without committing
- **Estimating campaign size and cost**: "If I target a 1-mile radius around this address, how many homes is that? How much will it cost?"
- **Comparing areas**: quickly evaluate two different neighborhoods side by side
- **Training new team members**: teach the platform without spending real campaign budget
- **Exploring a new market**: research a new geographic area before committing to a live campaign

---

## Sandbox Does Not Affect Live Data

Sandbox activity is completely separate from your live campaigns, analytics, and billing. Running sandbox sessions does not create zones, campaigns, or billing records in your main account. The only record is the $3.00 sandbox transaction in your billing history.
