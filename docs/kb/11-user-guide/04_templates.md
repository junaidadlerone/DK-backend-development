# User Guide: Templates

## Introduction

**Templates** are the postcard designs you mail to recipients. The Templates module lets you browse existing designs (your own, your agency's, and the shared library), create new ones, and customize them in a full drag‑and‑drop **editor** with a front and back.

This document covers:
- **A. The Templates list** — browsing and filtering.
- **B. Creating a new template** — name, canvas size, and opening the editor.
- **C. The Template Editor** — designing the postcard.

---

## A. The Templates List

### Steps

**Step 1: Open Templates**
1. Log in and choose your organization.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Templates**.
3. The Templates page opens with summary stats and a grid of templates.

**Step 2: Read the summary stats**
- **Total Templates**, **Active Templates**, **Draft Templates**, **Inactive Templates**.

**Step 3: Browse and filter**
- Switch sources with the tabs: **All Templates / Library Templates / Agency Templates / User Templates**.
- Filter by **Size**, **Created By**, and **Sort By**, or use **Search templates**.
- Each card shows the template name and its canvas size (e.g., **4 × 6**, **6 × 9**, **6 × 11**).

### Template sources

| Source | What it contains |
|--------|------------------|
| **Library** | Shared, ready‑made templates provided in the platform. |
| **Agency** | Templates created/owned at the agency level. |
| **User** | Templates you (the user) created. |

## B. Creating a New Template

### Steps
1. On the Templates page, click **Create New Template**.
2. In the **Create Template** dialog:
   - Enter a **Template Name**.
   - Choose a **Canvas Size**: **4 × 6**, **6 × 9**, or **6 × 11**.
3. Click **Create Template**.
4. The new template opens in the **editor** (see section C).

### Fields

| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|-------|------|:--------:|-------|
| Template Name | Text | ✔ | e.g., "Spring Promo 4x6". |
| Canvas Size | Choice | ✔ | 4 × 6 / 6 × 9 / 6 × 11 (inches). |

## C. The Template Editor

### Introduction

The editor is a full design canvas with a **Front** and **Back**. You add and arrange content using the left toolbar, preview the result, and save.

### Steps
1. Open a template (via **Create New Template**, **Open in Editor** during campaign creation, or by opening an existing template).
2. Use the **left toolbar** to add elements:
   - **Templates** — start from another design.
   - **Shapes**, **Content** (text), **Images**, **Icons**.
   - **Dynamic Text** — fields that auto‑fill (e.g., recipient/personalization).
   - **Layers** — manage stacking order of elements.
3. Switch between **Front** and **Back** using the toggle at the bottom.
4. Adjust the **Canvas Size** and **Fill** color from the right panel; zoom with the controls or **Fit to Screen**.
5. Use the **Editor / Preview** toggle at the top to preview the postcard.
6. Click **Save Template**. The header shows **"Just saved"** when changes are stored.

## Business & validation rules

- A template requires a **name** and a **canvas size** (4×6 / 6×9 / 6×11) to be created.
- Templates carry a status (**Active**, **Draft**, **Inactive**) reflected in the summary stats.
- When a template is used in a campaign via **Continue without Editing**, its text and images are **locked** for that campaign (you're warned before confirming).

## Finalization

After creating/editing a template:
- It is saved and appears in the **Templates** grid under the appropriate source tab.
- It becomes selectable in the **Pick a Template** step of campaign creation.

## Expected Result

The template is saved with the chosen canvas size and design, ready to be selected for postcard campaigns.
