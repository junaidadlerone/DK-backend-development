# Analytics Overview

The Analytics section gives you visibility into how your campaigns are performing — delivery rates, QR scan rates, cost efficiency, and engagement patterns.

## Accessing Analytics

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar. The page opens with four sub-tabs:

1. **Overview** — top-level KPIs across all campaigns
2. **Campaign Performance** — per-campaign breakdown
3. **ROI & Cost Analytics** — cost efficiency and return metrics
4. **Engagement Deep Dive** — geographic and temporal engagement data

---

## Overview Tab

The Overview tab shows aggregate performance metrics across all campaigns within the selected date range:

- **Total Postcards Sent**: cumulative count of all mailed postcards
- **Total Spend**: total amount charged across all campaigns
- **Delivery Rate**: percentage of sent postcards confirmed as delivered
- **QR Scan Rate**: percentage of sent postcards where the QR code was scanned

Use this tab to get a quick health check on your overall direct mail program.

---

## Campaign Performance Tab

A per-campaign breakdown showing:

- Campaign name and type
- Number of postcards sent
- Delivery rate for that campaign
- Number of QR scans
- Scan rate for that campaign
- Total spend

Sort by any column to find your best and worst performing campaigns.

---

## ROI & Cost Analytics Tab

Cost efficiency metrics:

- **Cost per postcard**: average across selected campaigns (same as price per postcard unless you've had coupon discounts)
- **Cost per lead**: total campaign spend ÷ leads generated (referrals that entered "In Use" status)
- **Estimated ROI**: calculated from your lead value estimate (configurable in Settings) vs total spend

---

## Engagement Deep Dive Tab

More detailed engagement data:

- **Time-of-scan breakdown**: when during the day/week QR codes are typically scanned
- **Geographic heatmap**: which areas have the highest scan rates
- **Scan trend over time**: how engagement evolves in the days after a mailing

---

## Date Range Filter

All four tabs respect the date range filter at the top of the page. The default is **Last 30 Days**. Change it to see all-time data, a specific month, or a custom range.

---

## Analytics Update Timing

Analytics data is **not real-time**. There is typically a **24–48 hour delay** after a campaign launches before delivery and scan data begins appearing. Full analytics data for a campaign may not be complete until 7–10 days after launch (once all postcards have been delivered).

---

## Analytics Are Organization-Scoped

If you have multiple organizations (agency workspace), each org has its own analytics. The analytics page shows data only for the currently active organization. Switch organizations using the top-bar org switcher to see a different org's data.
