# User Guide: Referrals

## Introduction

A **Referral** captures a past customer or completed job — their name, location, job type, and value — so you can build credibility‑boosting postcard campaigns around them ("we recently completed work near you"). Referrals are also the foundation of the **For Referrals** campaign type, where the referral's address becomes the campaign's primary location.

This document covers:
- **A. The Referrals list** — viewing and filtering referrals.
- **B. Adding a new referral** — the full form, field by field.

---

## A. The Referrals List

### Steps

**Step 1: Open Referrals**
1. Log in and choose your organization.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Referrals**.
3. The Referrals page opens with summary stats and a table.

**Step 2: Read the summary stats**
- **Total Referrals**
- **Campaign Ready Referrals** — referrals complete enough to use in a campaign.
- **Campaigns Published Referrals** — referrals already used in a published campaign.
- **Average Referrals Value**

**Step 3: Find a referral**
- Use **Search referrals** to search by name.
- Filter by **Job Type**, **Status**, and **All Values**, and sort with **Newest First**.
- The table shows **Referral Name (and phone), Address, Job Type, Job Value, Status, and Campaign Used**.

### Referral status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Ready** | The referral is complete and can be used to create a campaign. |

## B. Adding a New Referral

### Introduction

The **Add New Referral** form records the referrer's details, location, job information, and the **referrer's consent** to use their information in a referral campaign.

### Steps

**Step 1: Open the form**
1. On the Referrals page, click **+ Add New Referrals**.
2. The **Add New Referral** page opens.

**Step 2: Upload job images**
1. Under **Upload Job Images**, drag & drop or **Choose a file**.
2. Up to **10 images** are allowed (**JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, HEIF**, up to **25 MB** each).

**Step 3: Enter the referrer's details**
Complete the referrer name and contact fields (see field table).

**Step 4: Enter the location**
Enter the country, street address, city, state, and ZIP.

**Step 5: Enter job information**
Select the job type and enter the job value (and optional referral percentage and notes).

**Step 6: Confirm consent**
1. Tick the **Referrer Consent** checkbox confirming the referrer has given **written consent** to use their name, photos, and address for the referral campaign.

**Step 7: Save**
- Click **Create Referral** to save it as a usable referral, or **Save as Draft** to finish later.

### Fields

| Field | Type | Required | Notes / Example |
|-------|------|:--------:|-----------------|
| Upload Job Images | File upload | ✔ | Max 10 images; JPEG/PNG/WebP/HEIC/HEIF; ≤25 MB each. |
| Referrer Name | Text | ✔ | e.g., "Paul Walker". |
| Email | Email | ✖ | e.g., "paul@example.com". |
| Phone Number | Phone | ✖ | e.g., "+1 555 123 4567". |
| Country | Dropdown | ✔ | Defaults to "United States of America". |
| Street Address | Text | ✔ | e.g., "123 Main St". |
| City | Text | ✔ | e.g., "Atlanta". |
| State | Dropdown | ✔ | "Select a state". |
| ZIP | Text | ✔ | e.g., "67858". |
| Job Type | Dropdown | ✔ | e.g., AC Installation, AC Repair, AC Maintenance. |
| Job Value ($) | Number | ✔ | e.g., "100000.00". |
| Referral Percentage % | Number | ✖ | Optional referral percentage. |
| Notes | Text area (≤250 chars) | ✖ | Free‑text notes. |
| Referrer Consent | Checkbox | ✔ | Must be ticked — confirms written consent and agreement to referral terms. |

## Business & validation rules

- **Referrer Consent is mandatory** — you must confirm written consent before creating a referral.
- A complete referral becomes **Ready** and can be selected when creating a **For Referrals** campaign.
- Image uploads are capped at **10 files**, **25 MB** each, in supported formats only.
- **Notes** are limited to **250 characters**.

## Finalization

After saving:
- The referral appears in the Referrals list with its **status** (e.g., **Ready**) and **Campaign Used**.
- The summary stats (Total / Campaign Ready / Average Value) update accordingly.

## Expected Result

The referral is saved successfully and, once **Ready**, can be used as the basis (and primary location) for a **For Referrals** postcard campaign.
