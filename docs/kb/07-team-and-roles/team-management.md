# Team Management

## Viewing Your Team

Go to **Team** in the sidebar (or **Settings → Team / User Management**) to see all members of your organization with their names, emails, roles, and join dates.

---

## Inviting a Team Member

1. Click **+ Invite Member**
2. Enter the person's email address
3. Select their role (Admin, Marketer, or Technician)
4. Click **Send Invite**

The invited person will receive an email with a link to create their account and join your organization. If they already have a DoorKnocker account, they'll be prompted to accept the invitation.

**Note**: The Owner role cannot be assigned via invitation — it can only be transferred from the current owner.

---

## What Happens After an Invite Is Sent

- The invite appears as "Pending" in the team list until accepted
- The invited user has no access to your organization until they accept
- To resend the invite email, click the pending member's row → **Resend Invite**
- To cancel an invite before it's accepted, click the pending member's row → **Remove**

**Common issue**: invited user says they didn't receive the email. Ask them to check their spam/junk folder. If it's still not there, resend the invite from the team list.

---

## Changing a Team Member's Role

1. Find the member in the team list
2. Click the role badge next to their name
3. Select the new role from the dropdown
4. The change takes effect immediately

**Who can change roles:**
- Admins can change the roles of all members except the Owner
- The Owner can change anyone's role

---

## Removing a Team Member

1. Find the member in the team list
2. Click their row to open the member detail
3. Click **Remove Member**
4. Confirm the action

**Restrictions:**
- You cannot remove yourself
- You cannot remove the Owner
- Only Admins and Owners can remove members

Removed members immediately lose access to the organization. Their past actions and campaigns remain in the system.

---

## Ownership Transfer

To transfer your Owner role to another team member:

1. Go to **Settings → Organization** (or look for "Transfer Ownership" in Settings)
2. Select the team member you want to become the new Owner
3. Confirm the transfer

**This action is irreversible from the app.** Once transferred, you become an Admin. The new owner becomes the only person who can delete the organization or transfer ownership again. If you need to reverse an ownership transfer, contact support.

---

## Agency Team Members

If you're in an agency workspace, team members invited to the **agency organization** automatically get access to manage all client organizations under that agency. Team members invited directly to a **client organization** only have access to that specific client.

This means you can have agency-wide staff (who work across all clients) and client-specific staff (who only see one client's data).
