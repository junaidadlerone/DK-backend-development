# Billing and Payments

## Accessing Billing

Go to **Settings → Subscription/Payments** to manage payment methods and view billing history.

Only **Admin** and **Owner** roles can access the billing section. Marketers and Technicians do not see the billing tab.

---

## Adding a Payment Method

1. Click **+ Add Card**
2. Enter your credit or debit card details (card number, expiration date, CVV, billing address)
3. Click **Save**

The card is saved securely. Multiple payment methods can be on file.

---

## Setting a Default Payment Method

If you have multiple cards, set one as the default. The default card is charged when you launch a campaign.

To change the default:
1. Click the card you want to make default
2. Click **Set as Default**

---

## Removing a Payment Method

1. Click the card you want to remove
2. Click **Remove**

**You cannot remove the last payment method on file.** Add a new card first, set it as default, then remove the old one.

---

## Billing History

The billing history table shows all transactions for your organization:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Date | When the charge was made |
| Description | Campaign name or transaction type |
| Amount | Total charged |
| Status | Paid, Pending, or Failed |
| Invoice | Download link for the PDF invoice |

To download an invoice, click the receipt/download icon on the row. Invoices include itemized details (address count, price per postcard, coupon discount if any, total).

---

## Pricing Summary

| Paper Quality | Price Per Postcard |
|---|---|
| Standard | $3.00 |
| Premium | $3.50 |

You are charged only for **verified, deliverable addresses**. Invalid and unverified addresses are excluded from the charge.

There is no subscription fee, no minimum order, and no monthly commitment. You pay per campaign launch.

---

## Coupon Codes

Enter coupon codes at the campaign launch checkout step. The discount is applied to the total and shown as a line item on the invoice. Coupons cannot be applied retroactively to a campaign that has already launched.

---

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails at campaign launch:
- The campaign does not launch — no postcards are sent
- The campaign remains in Draft status
- The failure reason is shown on the review screen
- Update your payment method and try launching again

Common failure reasons: card expired, insufficient funds, card declined by issuer.

---

## Invoice Questions

Each invoice has a unique invoice number. If you need a copy of a past invoice for accounting purposes, download it from the Billing History table. If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact DoorKnocker support with the invoice number.
