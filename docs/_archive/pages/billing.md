# Billing & Subscription

**URL**: /settings → Billing tab

## Managing Your Plan
View current plan, usage, and upgrade options in Settings → Billing.

## Payment Methods
Payments are processed via Stripe. Update your card in the Billing section.

## Billing History
View and download past invoices from the Billing section.
Invoices are also emailed to the organization admin.
