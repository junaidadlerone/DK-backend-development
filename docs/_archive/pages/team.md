## Notes

## Roles
- **Admin**: Full access — org settings, billing, all campaigns, team management
- **Marketer**: Create/manage campaigns, zones, templates
- **Technician**: Limited operational access

## Inviting a Team Member
1. Click "Invite Member"
2. Enter their email address
3. Select a role (Admin, Marketer, Technician)
4. Click "Send Invite"
5. They receive an email invitation to create their account

## Managing Members
- View all active members and their roles
- Change role or remove a member from the member row actions
