## Notes

## Global Exclusions
Addresses excluded from all targeting zone searches.
Use for opt-out requests, do-not-mail lists, or your own business address.

### Adding an Exclusion
1. Click "Add Exclusion"
2. Enter the address
3. Save

Exclusions apply to future searches only — they don't retroactively affect saved zones.
