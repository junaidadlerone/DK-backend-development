# User Guide: Dashboard

## Introduction

The **Dashboard** is the first screen you see after selecting your organization. It gives you a single, at‑a‑glance view of how your postcard campaigns are performing — how many postcards are in flight, how many were delivered, your delivery rate, and your total spend — along with delivery funnels, a "waste meter" for at‑risk mail, scan trends over time, recent scans, and a campaign leaderboard.

Use the Dashboard to monitor overall health and to jump straight into creating a campaign or adding a referral.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Open the Dashboard
1. Log in to DoorKnocker Plus and choose your organization.
2. You are automatically taken to the **Dashboard** (or click **Dashboard** in the left sidebar at any time).

### Step 2: Choose a time range
1. Near the top, use the time filter to scope the metrics: **Last 24h**, **Last 7d**, **This Month**, or **All Time**.
2. You can also filter by **All Campaigns** to focus the figures.

### Step 3: Read the key metrics
The top row shows four headline cards:
- **In‑flight Postcards** — postcards currently being processed or in transit.
- **Postcards Delivered** — successfully delivered (with the number dropped shown beneath).
- **Delivery Rate** — percentage delivered.
- **Spend‑to‑Date** — total amount spent.

### Step 4: Review the Delivery Funnel and Exceptions
- The **Delivery Funnel** breaks the total postcards sent into stages: **Delivered, In Transit, Processed, Printing, Ready**.
- The **Exceptions** area shows **Returned** and **Cancelled** counts.

### Step 5: Check the Waste Meter
- The **Waste Meter** highlights money **At Risk** (e.g., cancelled, delayed >7 working days, or returned postcards) so you can act on wasted spend.

### Step 6: Explore trends and activity
- **Scan Trend** — a month‑by‑month chart of how many postcard QR codes were scanned.
- **Recent Scans** — the latest scan events with the address and time.
- **Campaign Leaderboard** — your top campaigns ranked by scan percentage and total scans.

### Step 7: Take quick action
- Click **+ Create New Campaign** to start a campaign, or **+ Add New Referral** to capture a referral — both without leaving the Dashboard.

## Finalization

After reviewing the Dashboard you will have:
- A clear picture of in‑flight vs. delivered postcards and your delivery rate.
- Visibility into spend and any **at‑risk** (wasted) money.
- An understanding of scan performance over time and which campaigns are leading.

## Expected Result

The Dashboard accurately reflects your organization's current postcard performance for the selected time range, and provides one‑click shortcuts to create a new campaign or add a referral.
