# What Is DoorKnocker?

DoorKnocker is a web platform that lets businesses send targeted direct mail postcard campaigns to specific neighborhoods and address lists — without needing a print vendor, a mailing house, or any design experience.

## Who It's For

DoorKnocker is built for:
- **Local service businesses**: roofing companies, HVAC contractors, landscapers, real estate agents, and any business where new customers come from a local geographic area
- **Marketing agencies**: manage campaigns for multiple client businesses from a single platform with full data isolation between clients

## The Core Problem It Solves

Traditional direct mail requires you to: find a mailing list vendor, design a postcard, hire a print shop, coordinate with a mail carrier, and track results manually. DoorKnocker collapses all of this into one workflow — you draw a zone on a map, design a postcard, and launch. Everything else is handled automatically.

## How It Works (High Level)

1. **Define a targeting zone** — draw a radius around an address, or upload your own address list
2. **Verify addresses** — the platform checks that each address is deliverable by a mail carrier
3. **Design a postcard** — use the built-in template editor to create a front and back design with your branding, offers, and a QR code
4. **Launch the campaign** — pay per verified address, and your postcards enter the print and mail queue
5. **Track results** — monitor delivery rates, QR code scans, and estimated ROI in the analytics dashboard

## What DoorKnocker Does NOT Do

- No digital advertising (no social media ads, no email campaigns, no SMS)
- No phone calls or outbound sales
- No physical door-to-door canvassing
- DoorKnocker is exclusively for physical postcards sent through the mail

## Key Capabilities

- **Geographic precision**: target addresses within a specific radius of any point, down to 0.1 miles
- **QR code tracking**: each campaign gets a unique QR code so you can measure scan rates
- **Referral linking**: connect campaigns to completed job sites to target the surrounding neighborhood
- **Verified deliverability**: only pay for addresses that a mail carrier can actually deliver to
- **Sandbox mode**: test campaign targeting and pricing estimates without spending real money
