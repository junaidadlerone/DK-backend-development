# Agency Overview

The Agency workspace is designed for marketing agencies that manage direct mail campaigns for multiple client businesses. It gives you a single login to oversee all your clients without mixing their data.

## The Agency Overview Page

Go to **Agency → Overview** to see a grid of all your client organizations. Each card shows:
- Client organization name
- Status (active or inactive)
- Number of campaigns
- Quick-access button to switch into that client's workspace

This page is your command center for monitoring all clients at a glance.

---

## Creating a Client Organization

1. From the Agency Overview page, click **+ Add Organization**
2. A new organization onboarding flow starts — fill in the client's business details:
   - Business name
   - Industry
   - Business address
   - Branding (logo and colors)
   - (Optional) Invite team members for this client
3. After completing onboarding, the new client org appears in your Agency Overview

---

## Switching Between Clients

Two ways to switch into a client's workspace:

1. **Agency Overview card**: click the client's card → you're taken directly into that org
2. **Top-bar org switcher**: click the organization name in the top navigation bar → a dropdown lists your agency org and all client orgs → click the one you want

When you switch, the entire sidebar, campaigns list, zones, templates, and analytics update to reflect the selected organization. Your login session stays the same.

---

## Agency vs Client Data Isolation

Each client organization is completely isolated:
- Campaigns created in Client A are not visible in Client B
- Team members added to Client A only see Client A's data (unless they were also added to Client B)
- Billing for Client A is separate from Client B
- Analytics for Client A are separate from Client B

The only shared layer is your agency-level team and agency-level templates.

---

## Agency Team

Agency-level team members have access to manage all client organizations under your agency. Add them at **Agency → Team**.

Client-specific team members should be added from within the individual client organization.

---

## Agency Templates

Create postcard templates at the agency level (**Agency → Templates**) that can be shared with any of your client organizations. This lets you build a design once and deploy it across multiple clients. See the Agency Client Management guide for how to share templates.
